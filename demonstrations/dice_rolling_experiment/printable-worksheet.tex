\subsection{Purpose}\label{purpose}

Use a fair six-sided die to investigate chance behaviour. Complete the
tasks below after making observations from a class simulator, physical
die, or other approved random experiment.

\subsection{Part 1: Prediction}\label{part-1-prediction}

Before you start, answer the following questions.

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Which face do you think will appear most often after many rolls?
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{1.8cm}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\setcounter{enumi}{1}
\tightlist
\item
  Do you think the frequencies will be exactly equal? Explain why or why
  not.
\end{enumerate}
